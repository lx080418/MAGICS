Portola Springs Elementary School

Portola Springs Elementary School is a public school which opened in the fall of 2015. The school is a TK-6th Grade Elementary School within Irvine Unified School District and currently has approximately 850 students. Since its after-school organization, Rising Rainbow, partnered with MAGIC Math in January 2026, the program has provided over seven hundred student participations, offering a richer, more engaging mathematics learning experience and helping students enhance their skills. It has received high praise from teachers, students, and parents.
